Typical targets: LDL below 3.0 mmol/L (115 mg/dL) if you have no other risk factors, and below 1.8 mmol/L (70 mg/dL) if you have heart disease or diabetes. Your own target depends on your overall risk.

This is an estimate, not a diagnosis. Some labs measure LDL directly, which is more accurate, especially when triglycerides are high. Always discuss your results with your doctor.
